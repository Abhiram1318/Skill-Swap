How SkillSwap works

SkillSwap makes learning social. Instead of only watching tutorials, meet people who know something you want to learn and teach them something you know.

1
🧑‍🎓

Create your student profile

Tell the community your age group, location, skills, interests and availability.

2
🔎

Discover people

Find learners interested in coding, languages, academics, sports, arts, music and more.

3
✨

Find a smart match

SkillSwap compares what you want to learn with what another person can teach.

4
🤝

Request a swap

Offer your own skill in return and explain what you'd like to learn.

5
💬

Chat safely

Use the platform to discuss your learning goals and arrange an appropriate learning session.

6
⭐

Learn and grow

Complete the exchange, leave feedback and help build a friendly learning community.
