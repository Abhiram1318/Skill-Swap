How SkillSwap works

SkillSwap makes learning social. Meet people who know something you want to learn and teach them something you know.

1
🧑‍🎓

Create your profile

Tell the community your age group, location, skills, interests and availability.

🔎

Discover people

Find learners interested in coding, languages, academics, sports, arts and music.

✨
